Physical properties of a total energy / interatomic potential model:

    * finite differences of energy with respect to atomic positions should approximate forces
    * systems with perturbed atomic positions should relax to roughly the same energy and atomic positions
    * relaxations should be stable and not diverge
    * total energy per atom should be constant as number of atoms increases when creating supercells

Expected properties of a bilayer graphene system:

    * equilibrium layer separation for AB stacking bilayer graphene should be around 3.4 +/- 0.1 Å (depending on model)
    * AA stacking should have layer equilbrium interlayer separations of 3.6 +/- 0.1 Å 
    * relaxed twisted bilayer graphene systems should have equilbrium interlayer separations that vary from AA stacking layer separations to AB stacking layer separations (within margins of error)
    * layer separation at minimum energy for bilayer graphene system with no strain (lattice constant = 2.46+/-0.04) should equal layer separation found for a relaxed system 
    * the atoms in each graphene layer should have roughly the same z coordinate (no inplane buckling expected)
